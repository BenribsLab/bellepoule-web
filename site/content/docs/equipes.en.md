## Match types

The fencers and substitutes of a team are set once and for all at [check-in](@/docs/appel.md). Each phase then chooses its match type:

| Type | Top | Bottom | Relay order |
|---|---|---|---|
| Everyone against everyone, 3 vs 3 | 1-2-3 | 4-5-6 | 3-6, 1-5, 2-4, 1-6, 3-4, 2-5, 1-4, 2-6, 3-5 |
| Everyone against everyone, 2 vs 2 | 1-2 | 3-4 | 1-4, 2-3, 1-3, 2-4 |
| Simple relay (one bout per fencer) | 1-3-5… | 2-4-6… | 1-2, 3-4, 5-6… |

The score is **cumulative**: relay *n* ends when a team reaches *n* × (points per relay), or at the end of the relay time. With 5-point relays and 3 vs 3, the match is fenced to 45 touches. A tie at the end is decided by the referee.

## At the start of the match

Teams choose their order and give it to the referee: nothing is pre-filled. The referee sets which team is at the **top** and which at the **bottom**, then the fencer of each number.

## Match sheets

- **Blank**, in any quantity, or **those of the phase's matches** (team names);
- one at a time or **by 3**: one for each team and one for the referee;
- **filled in** as soon as the referee has entered the line-up on the phone: teams placed, fencers, cumulative scores, touches and each fencer's balance.

## Entry on the phone

With [ManageComp Referee](@/docs/arbitre.md), the referee follows the relay order: "Relay 4/9: 1 against 6", cumulative scores, relay timer, end of relay (at the score or on time). The result is sent at the end of the match.

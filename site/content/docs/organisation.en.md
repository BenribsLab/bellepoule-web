The organisation shows the **hall** (the pistes), the **timeline**, the list of **bouts to place** and the **referees**.

## Place bouts

- The list shows each pool or bout's state: **To place** (at the top), Placed, Referees only.
- **Place…** automatically spreads a batch's bouts over free pistes from the timeline cursor.
- You can also drag a bout onto a piste, or double-click a piste or a pool to set its **time** by hand.

## Pistes and referees of a pool

A pool can use several pistes and have several referees. Referees are spread over the pistes; on a piste with several referees, they take turns bout after bout.

## Place a group

For youth training, **Place a group…** places all the pools of a group together on the chosen pistes ([Youth training](@/docs/entrainement-jeunes.md)). Only referees free during the group are offered.

## Referees from the same club

Assigning a referee from the same club as a fencer shows a message to confirm: "Referee from the same club as a fencer… Assign anyway?". When spreading bouts, a referee does not referee a fencer of their club as long as another referee of the piste is free.

## Main table and placing tables

The main table is seeded from the previous phase's ranking. Depending on the settings, all places are fenced, only 3rd place, or none: losers then go into placing tables.

## Score entry

{{ figure(src="tableau.png", caption="The direct elimination table in progress.") }}

Click a bout and enter each fencer's score. If time runs out before the maximum score, type `W` followed by the winner's touches (`W12` for a 12–10 win). The winner moves forward in the table.

## Match sheets

Match sheets are printed for ready bouts (both fencers known), with touch boxes to tick and, when referees are connected to their phone, the bout's **QR code** (scan it, then the referee's PIN code). For a team event, these are the [relay sheets](@/docs/equipes.md).

To find a bout in a large table, type its number in the **Search** box (software) or **Go to bout no.** (online version): `3` for bout 1-16.3.

If the competition allows it, **⚠ Automatic filling** draws random scores for the remaining bouts of the displayed table, to test the formula ([First competition](@/docs/premiere-competition.md)).

## Barrage

In case of a tie for qualification, a **barrage** is fenced like a table bout, with its own match sheet.

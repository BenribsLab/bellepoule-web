## Composition

{{ figure(src="poules-composition.png", caption="Pool composition: suggested formulas and fencer distribution.") }}

ManageComp suggests the possible pool formulas (number and size). Fencers are spread in a serpentine according to their ranking, with **separation** by club, league, region or country so that fencers of the same club do not meet in pools. Fencers can be moved between pools by drag and drop.

**Validating the composition** creates the bouts; sheets can then be printed and pools placed on pistes.

## Score entry

{{ figure(src="poules-saisie.png", caption="A pool: score grid, statuses, ranking and list of bouts.") }}

Click a cell of the grid and type the score: `5` or `V` for the winner, the number of touches for the loser.

If the bout stops at the end of time before the winner reaches the maximum score, type `W` followed by their touches: `W3` for a 3–2 win. The grid then shows `V3`.

**Double defeat** (pool time over): enter the same score for both fencers, `0` and `0` if the bout did not start, or `3` and `3` for a tie. Shortcut: `D` (0-0) or `D3` (3-3) in either cell. The bout is over with no winner; the touches count in the indicators. On the referee's phone, a "Double defeat" button appears on a tie. Each fencer's menu shows their status: qualified, withdrawn, excluded, forfeit. This menu only changes on click (the mouse wheel scrolls the page).

Referees can also enter their bouts on their phone ([Referees on their phone](@/docs/arbitre.md)).

## Several pistes and referees

A pool can be fenced on several pistes at once, with one or more referees. Bouts are spread so that a fencer never has two bouts at the same time; on a piste with several referees, **they take turns bout after bout**, avoiding fencers of their own club. The pool sheet shows the piste and referee of each bout.

## Ranking

The pool ranking (victories, index, touches scored) and the round ranking update with each score.

## Printing

Pool sheets for referees (blank) or with results, one pool or all of them.

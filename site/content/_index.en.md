## Free software, heir to BellePoule

ManageComp faithfully follows **BellePoule**, the reference fencing competition software created by Yannick Le Roux, and extends it: online version, score entry by referees on their phones, team relays, youth training.

Like BellePoule, ManageComp is **free software** under the GNU GPL: you may use, study, modify and redistribute it freely. Competition files (`.cotcot`) remain compatible with BellePoule.
